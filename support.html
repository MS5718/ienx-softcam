常見問題

臉型、化妝或證件照一直顯示「偵測臉部中」？

第一次使用需要網路下載臉部偵測模型（約 13 MB），請確認網路連線後按「重試」。下載過一次之後就會加快。

照片會被上傳嗎？

不會。所有處理都在你的手機上進行，詳見 隱私權政策。

證件照可以直接拿去用嗎？

本 App 依公開的規格製作尺寸，但各單位規定可能不同；換背景或修圖的照片部分單位可能不接受，送件前請先確認。

沖印 4×6 沖印版要怎麼選？

在超商或相館選擇 4×6 吋、原尺寸列印，不要裁切或縮放。

影片方向不對？

在影片畫面按右上角的「旋轉」，App 會記住你的選擇。

FAQ

Face shape, makeup or ID photo keeps saying “Finding the face”?

The first use needs internet to download the face detection model (about 13 MB). Check your connection and tap Retry.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use the ID photos as they are?

Sizes follow published specifications, but requirements vary and some offices may not accept photos with a replaced background or retouching. Please check before submitting.

How do I print the 4×6 sheet?

Choose 4×6 in at original size — no cropping or scaling.

Video is the wrong way round?

Tap Rotate at the top right of the video screen. The app remembers your choice.
